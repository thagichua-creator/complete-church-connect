Sanctuary modernism: a trusted church operations workspace with the quiet dignity of a contemporary sanctuary and the precision of a well-kept parish register. Light mode first, with a complete dark mode. Compact-to-generous pacing: information-dense enough for administrators, calm and breathable for everyday use. Deep navy is the structural anchor; muted gold is a restrained signal for primary actions, selected states and meaningful highlights, never decoration everywhere. Warm ivory surfaces sit above a pale blue-grey canvas; fine borders, subtle layered shadows and modest rounded corners create a crafted, dependable feel. Use a distinctive editorial serif for page headings paired with a highly legible neutral sans for interface text; tabular numerals for counts and reports. Keep charts restrained and accessible, and use shape, labels and position as well as color to communicate status.
